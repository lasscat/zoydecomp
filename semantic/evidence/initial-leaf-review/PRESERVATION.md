# Reconstruction evidence

The verified source is under src/auto/, with compiler/source bindings in
config/BWBE-r0/build.json and accepted results in accepted.json. The research bank
contains fourteen additional leaf drafts and the RNG seed draft, with portable
metadata and current measurements in semantic/manifest.json.

reviewed-42.json records candidate boundaries, caller evidence and uncertainty.
The current discovery inventory is config/BWBE-r0/functions-v2.json. These are
partial observations, not a complete code/data map or full-game denominator.

worker3-results.json preserves initial hypotheses and measurements. Its historical
summary differs from the authoritative reproduced results: use semantic/manifest.json
for current tiers. Compiler-summary.json preserves bounded compiler identities and
seed results; unprobed-leads.json contains ideas that were not executed.

Obsolete launch scripts, machine paths, duplicate discovery dumps and raw scheduling
logs are excluded from the public snapshot. Their original records are preserved
in the private development archive. Reproduction uses tracked sources and metadata,
not those logs or private scratch paths.
